[[Welkom by Hoërskool Dinamika||Welcome to Hoërskool Dinamika]]

“Reik na die Lig”

Ons skoolleuse motiveer ons om nie tevrede te wees met middelmatigheid nie, maar eerder om hoër te reik, verder te gaan, harder te probeer en altyd na uitnemendheid te mik.

Our school motto motivates us not to settle for mediocrity, but to reach higher, go further, push harder and always aim for excellence.

Ons handel nie in die donker nie, maar wandel in die lig. Ons reik na die lig by Dinamika. By Namies is ons trots op ons skooltradisies, etos en toeganklikheid — 'n plek waar elke leerder gesien en ontwikkel word.

We do not operate in the dark, but walk in the light. We reach for the light at Dinamika. At Namies we take pride in our school traditions, ethos and approachability — a place where every learner is seen and developed.

    [[Hoe om aansoek te doen||How to apply]]

    [[Alle graad 8-plasings word deur die Gauteng Departement van Onderwys gedoen. Vir graad 9 tot 12 doen u direk by die skool aansoek.||All Grade 8 placements are done by the Gauteng Department of Education. For Grades 9 to 12 you apply directly at the school.]]

    [[Graad 8 in vier stappe||Grade 8 in four steps]]

    1. [[Doen aanlyn aansoek||Apply online]][[Ouers doen aanlyn aansoek by die Gauteng Departement van Onderwys (GDE).||Parents apply online with the Gauteng Department of Education (GDE).]]
    2. [[Sertifiseer dokumente||Certify documents]][[ID of paspoort, geboortesertifikaat, bewys van adres en die leerder se graad 7-rapport.||ID or passport, birth certificate, proof of address and the learner's Grade 7 report.]]
    3. [[Handig by die skool in||Submit at the school]][[Handig die aansoekvorm en die oorspronklike gesertifiseerde dokumente by die skool in.||Hand in the application form and the original certified documents at the school.]]
    4. [[Plasing & korrespondensie||Placement & correspondence]][[Ná plasing deur die departement ontvang ouers verdere korrespondensie van die skool.||After placement by the department, parents receive further correspondence from the school.]]
