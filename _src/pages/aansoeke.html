[[Graad 8-aansoeke||Grade 8 applications]]

[[Graad 8 by Dinamika||Grade 8 at Dinamika]]

[[Alle graad 8-plasings word deur die Gauteng Departement van Onderwys (GDE) gedoen. Hoërskool Dinamika en die GDE verifieer al die dokumente.||All Grade 8 placements are done by the Gauteng Department of Education (GDE). Hoërskool Dinamika and the GDE verify all the documents.]]

  1. [[Doen aanlyn aansoek by die GDE||Apply online with the GDE]][[Ouers doen aanlyn aansoek by die Gauteng Departement van Onderwys en voltooi die aansoekvorm volledig.||Parents apply online with the Gauteng Department of Education and complete the application form in full.]]
  2. [[Laat dokumente sertifiseer||Have documents certified]][[Sien die lys van vereiste dokumente, plus die leerder se graad 7-skoolrapport.||See the list of required documents, plus the learner's Grade 7 school report.]]
  3. [[Handig by die skool in||Submit at the school]][[Handig die aansoekvorm sowel as die oorspronklike gesertifiseerde dokumente by die skool in.||Hand in the application form as well as the original certified documents at the school.]]
  4. [[Plasing||Placement]][[Ná plasing deur die departement ontvang ouers verdere korrespondensie van die skool.||After placement by the department, parents receive further correspondence from the school.]]

[[Vereiste dokumente||Required documents]]

[[Gesertifiseerde dokumente||Certified documents]]

[[Die volgende dokumente moet gesertifiseer word en saam met die oorspronklikes by die skool ingehandig word.||The following documents must be certified and submitted at the school together with the originals.]]

  • {{i:check}}[[ID of paspoort||ID or passport]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Suid-Afrikaanse geboortesertifikaat||South African birth certificate]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Bewys van huisadres: munisipale rekening nie ouer as 3 maande nie, in die naam van die aansoeker se ouer||Proof of residence: municipal account not older than 3 months, in the name of the applicant's parent]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Enige rekening in die naam van die aansoeker met die volle fisiese adres||Any statement of account in the applicant's name with the full physical address]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Bewys van werksadres, indien onder werksadres aansoek gedoen word||Proof of work address, if applying under a work address]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Graad 8: die leerder se graad 7-rapport · Graad 9–12: nuutste rapport en portefeuljes||Grade 8: the learner's Grade 7 report · Grade 9–12: latest report and portfolios]]

[[Huurders||Tenants]]

[[Indien u 'n huurder is, voeg ook die volgende by:||If you are renting, please also add the following:]]

  • {{i:check}}[[Munisipale rekening (nie ouer as 3 maande nie) in die naam van die huiseienaar||Municipal account (not older than 3 months) in the landlord's name]]
  • {{i:check}}[[ID van die huiseienaar||Landlord's ID]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Huurooreenkoms geteken deur huurder en verhuurder||Lease agreement signed by tenant and landlord]]
  • {{i:check}}[[Huurbetalingstrokie nie ouer as 3 maande nie||Rental payment slip not older than 3 months]]

[[Skoolfonds||School fees]]

[[Die skoolfonds word elke November by die algemene jaarvergadering vir die volgende jaar bepaal. Die bedrae hieronder is die mees onlangs gepubliseerde bedrae — kontak asseblief die rekeningekantoor om die huidige jaar se bedrae te bevestig.||School fees are set every November at the annual general meeting for the following year. The amounts below are the most recently published amounts — please contact the accounts office to confirm the current year's amounts.]]

[[Skoolfonds per kind||School fees per child]]
[[Kind||Child]][[Per jaar||Per year]]
[[Eerste kind||First child]]R25 000,00
[[Tweede kind||Second child]]R25 000,00
[[Derde kind||Third child]]R12 500,00
[[Vierde kind of meer||Fourth child or more]][[GRATIS||FREE]]

[[Of R2 500,00 per kind per maand vir 10 maande. Skoolgeld sluit nie akademiese, sport- en kultuurtoere in nie — hierdie aktiwiteite is vrywillig.||Or R2 500.00 per child per month for 10 months. School fees do not include academic, sport and cultural tours — these activities are voluntary.]]

[[Deposito||Deposit]]

[['n Deposito van R2 500,00 is jaarliks voor 30 November betaalbaar vir huidige en nuwe leerders. Dit word van die verskuldigde skoolfonds afgetrek en die balans is oor 10 maande betaalbaar.||A deposit of R2 500.00 is payable annually before 30 November for current and new learners. It is deducted from the school fees due and the balance is payable over 10 months.]]

[[Afslag vir vroeë betaling||Early-payment discount]]

[[Op die vasgestelde bedrag van R25 000,00:||On the set amount of R25 000.00:]]

  • [[Ten volle betaal voor of op 31 Desember||Paid in full before or on 31 December]]10%
  • [[Ten volle betaal voor of op 31 Januarie||Paid in full before or on 31 January]]7,5%
  • [[Ten volle betaal voor of op 31 Maart||Paid in full before or on 31 March]]5%
